Play Designer

Diagram plays step by step, animate them, organize your playbook and print it. Plays save in this browser.

Gold = offenseWhite = defenseSolid arrow = cutWavy = dribbleDashed = passT-bar = screenDotted = shot

How it works: draw cuts, passes and screens starting on a player, then + Add step. The next step starts with everyone where their line ended and the ball with the pass receiver. Animate plays the whole sequence. Double-click a player to rename her label (PG, 4...).
